Patrol Toolkit Support

Updated: May 1, 2026

For support requests, email us and include your device model, iOS or Android version, and a short description of the issue.

Patrol Toolkit is a local-first app. Most operational content stays on the device unless you choose to export, share, or use a limited online helper service described in the Privacy Policy.

Typical Response Time

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Common Issues

  • App not loading: close and reopen the app, then restart your device.
  • Data not appearing: confirm you are in the correct feature section and not filtered by date/state.
  • Lifetime unlock access: use Restore Purchases inside the app, then relaunch Patrol Toolkit.
  • App lock not opening: confirm the device has a passcode, PIN, Face ID, or fingerprint configured in system settings.

Privacy and Purchase Notes

  • The current build does not request device GPS location permission.
  • If you use pursuit address search or route matching, user-entered addresses or waypoints may be sent to mapping providers for app functionality.
  • Premium features are unlocked through a one-time non-renewing lifetime purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Purchase verification and restore support are handled through RevenueCat using store receipt information and anonymous app user identifiers.
